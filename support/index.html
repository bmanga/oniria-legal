Oniria Support

We usually reply within two business days: support@oniria.app

  • Restore a purchase: open the paywall (Settings → Upgrade) and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple ID you subscribed with.
  • Cancel or change your subscription: on your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Oniria. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge.
  • Free trial: you won't be charged if you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com
  • Back up your journal: Settings → Backup & Restore creates an encrypted file you can save to Files or iCloud Drive.
  • Delete your data: Settings → Delete All Data removes everything stored on your device.
  • Privacy Policy: https://bmanga.github.io/oniria-legal/privacy/
  • Terms of Use: https://bmanga.github.io/oniria-legal/terms/

For anything else, email support@oniria.app
